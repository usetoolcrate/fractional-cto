The questions everyone asks

What kinds of problems do you help solve?

Usually, it begins with a website that no longer reflects the business or no longer does a useful job. The work may include a rebuild, a better lead or customer path, integrations between systems, commerce or booking flows, internal tools, or carefully chosen automation.

I do not sell a pre-made technology package. We start with the business issue and decide whether the right answer is a repair, a project, or no change at all.

Do you take ownership of our website or accounts?

No. Your business keeps control of its domain, accounts, data, and website. My role is to take responsibility for the technical work we agree on, not to take possession of your assets.

Where a handoff is appropriate, the work is documented so the next qualified person can understand it.

How do we know whether this is a fit?

We start with a conversation about the business, the website, and the result you need. If there is a useful fit, I will recommend a defined next step. If the work belongs with a different specialist—or the current setup does not need changing—I will tell you that directly.

Do you work with our existing vendors and software?

Yes. The point is to make the existing business work better, not to force a replacement of every tool you use. I will map the systems involved, identify the constraints, and recommend changes only where they make sense.

What does a 90-day engagement look like?

First, we clarify the bottleneck and define the outcome. Then I work through the website and systems that affect it, ship the agreed improvements, and leave you with documentation and a sensible next-step plan. The exact scope is written down before the engagement begins.

What happens after a project is complete?

You can take the work and use another qualified person, or we can continue with a defined technical-partner arrangement if that is useful. Either way, you retain control of your business assets and there is no surprise obligation.
